About the Author

Fourteen years in the classroom and still asking better questions

I teach high school English and Humanities at Westbridge Academy, where I've spent the better part of my career obsessing over how people learn to think and write — and how to make that process visible to students while they're in it.

This blog is where I think out loud. I write about pedagogy, curriculum design, the books I teach and why, assessment practices that don't feel punitive, and the strange daily comedy of secondary education.

I hold an M.Ed. from Columbia Teachers College and a B.A. in English Literature from the University of Michigan. I've presented at NCTE conferences, served as department chair, and once accidentally introduced a Socratic seminar on Beloved while eating a granola bar.
